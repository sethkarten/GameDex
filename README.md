# GameDex

GameDex records your Pokémon Emerald sessions in mGBA and uploads them so we can build a world model that learns from how people actually play. You need a personal invite code, which you get by email after filling in the sign-up form.

Setup takes about 10 minutes. Then you just play.

## Windows setup

1. **Download GameDex**
   Grab the latest `GameDex_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe` from the [releases page](https://github.com/sethkarten/GameDex/releases/latest) and run it. If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click **More info** then **Run anyway**. Everything needed for recording is included, no extra installs.
2. **Enter your invite code**
   Open GameDex and paste the personal invite code from your email on the welcome screen. Device name is optional.
3. **Install mGBA**
   Download it from [mgba.io](https://mgba.io/downloads.html) and install it. This is the emulator that runs the game.
4. **Get your Pokémon Emerald ROM**
   Open your own legally obtained [Pokémon Emerald ROM](https://www.google.com/search?q=Pok%C3%A9mon+-+Emerald+Version+(USA%2C+Europe)) in mGBA. We do not provide game files ourselves. Windowed mode is fine. Just do not minimise the mGBA window while recording.
5. **Record**
   On the **Record** tab in GameDex, pick the mGBA window from the list (click **Refresh** if it is not there) and click **Start Recording**.
6. **Play, then stop**
   Play for about 60 to 90 minutes, then click **Stop Recording**. Recordings are saved to `Documents\GameDex Recordings`.
7. **Upload**
   Open the **Sessions** tab and click **Upload** on your session, or **Upload All**. That is it.

## macOS setup

1. **Download GameDex**
   You need **macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer**. Download `GameDex-macOS-unsigned.zip` from the [releases page](https://github.com/sethkarten/GameDex/releases/latest), unzip it, and move `GameDex.app` into your **Applications** folder.
2. **Open GameDex for the first time**
   Double-click GameDex. macOS will say *"Apple could not verify GameDex is free of malware that may harm your Mac"*. That is expected, the app is not notarised yet. Click **Done**, then allow it once:
   1. Open **System Settings**.
   2. Click **Privacy & Security** in the sidebar.
   3. Scroll down to the **Security** section until you see the note about GameDex being blocked.
   4. Click **Open Anyway**, then confirm again that you trust it (your Mac may ask for your password or Touch ID).
   5. Your Mac remembers this choice. From now on GameDex opens normally.
3. **Enter your invite code**
   Open GameDex and paste the personal invite code from your email on the welcome screen.
4. **Install mGBA**
   Download it from [mgba.io](https://mgba.io/downloads.html) and install it. This is the emulator that runs the game.
5. **Get your Pokémon Emerald ROM**
   Open your own legally obtained [Pokémon Emerald ROM](https://www.google.com/search?q=Pok%C3%A9mon+-+Emerald+Version+(USA%2C+Europe)) in mGBA. We do not provide game files ourselves.
6. **Record**
   Select the mGBA window in GameDex and click **Start Recording** (if macOS asks, allow **Screen Recording** and **Accessibility** or **Input Monitoring**, then reopen GameDex).
7. **Play, then stop**
   Play for about 60 to 90 minutes, then stop the recording. Recordings are saved to `Documents/GameDex Recordings`.
8. **Upload**
   Open the **Sessions** tab and click **Upload**, or **Upload All**. Done.

## Before you record

- 720p or higher at 30 fps
- Original 3:2 aspect ratio
- Normal shader
- English language
- Original game, no ROM hacks

## Tips for a good session

- Aim for 60 to 90 minutes per session.
- Play normally. Battles, exploring, menus, shopping, all of it is useful.
- Keep going where you left off. Sessions from later in the game are the most valuable, so save your game and come back.
- You can upload right after playing or later. Sessions wait in the Sessions tab until you upload them.
- Your invite code works on up to 3 of your devices, so please keep it to yourself.

## Something not working?

Reply to your invite email with a screenshot and your operating system, or write to pokemonworldrl@gmail.com. We usually answer the same day.
